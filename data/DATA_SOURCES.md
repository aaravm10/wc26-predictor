# Data Sources

## Instructions
Download the following datasets and place them in `data/raw/`.
These files are gitignored and must be downloaded manually.

## Datasets

### 1. International Football Results (PRIMARY)
- URL: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/martj42/international-football-results-from-1872-to-2017
- Files needed: results.csv, shootouts.csv, goalscorers.csv, former_names.csv
- Place in: data/raw/
